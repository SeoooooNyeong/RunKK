#### 15.4.6 배틀런 랜덤 매칭 (WatchFlowViewModel.startMatching·noticeOf·refusedPicker, watch.battle WatchBattleFlow.startMatching·awaitMatch·defaultDistance·runsLeft, D-376·D-413·D-416)
